Welcome to Faculty of Engineering

Faculty of Engineering, Dayalbagh Educational Institute, which is widely recognized as one of the most prestigious engineering institutions of India, was founded by His Holiness Param Guru Huzur Mehtaji Maharaj in 1950 as Dayalbagh Engineering College in response to the country's needs for trained technical manpower.

It became a part of the Dayalbagh Educational Institute (Deemed University) in the year 1981. The Faculty has held aloft the beacon of knowledge, research and education in the field of engineering throughout its existence. Its innovative and comprehensive scheme of education aims at excellence but not at the cost of relevance, encourages initiative and creative work, which is inter-disciplinary and which prepares the student for the increasingly techno-oriented society of tomorrow. The mission objective of the value oriented educational system is to evolve a 'Complete Man', i.e., a well rounded person whose hallmarks are intellectual strength, emotional maturity, truthfulness, general awareness, devotion to duty, high moral character, simple living, scientific temperament and societal commitment.

Training & Placement Cell

The Faculty Training and Placement Cell (FTPC) co-ordinates with various industries and organizations for Summer Training, Five Months Cooperative Education Program with Industries (Internship) apart from Campus Recruitment.

FTPC comprises several faculty members and a group of dynamic Student Representatives from various classes to assist the training and placement officer.

It also coordinates with the placement cell of the DEI Technical College for placement of diploma students and various Alumni associations of DEI.
